# Shared Research

Evidence cards are catalyst-linked factual observations. The author explicitly selects Supports Bull, Supports Bear, Neutral, or Needs Verification; OJ never infers the label. Cards may include a source link, confidence, affected assumption, and verification state. Members can comment, confirm, challenge, or provide a counter-source.

A Share Thesis dialog previews the exact outbound record: ticker, bias, normalized strategy, reviewed summary, optional expected move, and optional confidence. Candidate strikes, debit, contracts, maximum risk, account limits, entry/exit records, and private notes are excluded by both the UI and schema.

Members can respond to a shared thesis or fork it. Forking creates a new private Idea owned by the current user. The fork stores provenance but is independent: later private edits are never sent back to the workspace, and later shared edits never overwrite the private Idea.
