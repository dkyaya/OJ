# Forecast Calibration

Insights classify only the current user's locked forecasts that have a debrief with an actual direction. Drafts, forecasts without outcomes, and another member's viewpoints are excluded.

Direction accuracy is the count of exact bullish, bearish, neutral, or mixed matches divided by classified forecasts. Average confidence is the arithmetic mean of the confidence entered at lock time. Magnitude error is mean absolute error only where both forecast and outcome provide numeric magnitude.

OJ displays **Not Enough Forecasts Yet** below three classified forecasts. The sample count and definitions remain visible. These descriptive measures are not investment advice, a leaderboard, or evidence of future performance.
