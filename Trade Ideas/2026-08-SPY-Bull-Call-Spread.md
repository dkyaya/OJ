# SPY Bull Call Spread — August 2026

> **Watchlist only.** This is a decision journal, not an order or a record of execution.

SPY is expected to trend upward through late August if it holds or recovers following the jobs reaction. Market interpretation matters more than the headline alone. Any position must be confirmed after the August 7 report and closed before NVIDIA earnings on August 26.

- SPY holds or recovers after the initial reaction; yields are stable or declining.
- Soft-landing interpretation remains intact and post-report support holds.
- Both legs have acceptable liquidity and combined bid/ask width.
- Spread cost remains inside defined risk.
